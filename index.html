About Elite Central Portal

Elite Batch 2025–2029 • Francis Xavier Engineering College

Portal Mission & Purpose

Elite Central Portal acts as the unified landing hub for students of the Elite Batch (2025–2029). It aggregates attendance records, task planning matrices, task submissions, problem-solving daily updations, and hackathon competition evidence in one accessible destination.

EVIDERA — Proof Submission Guidelines

EVIDERA is the dedicated external portal where students upload and verify screenshots as proof of participating in hackathons, coding contests, ideathons, and project presentations.

  • Ensure screenshots show your registered student name, team name, and date of event.
  • Files should be clear PNG or JPG captures of the submission confirmation or leaderboard.
  • Direct link: https://student-screenshot-portal.onrender.com/hackathons

Security & Independence Notice

This central portal is a client-side navigation system deployed on Vercel. Connected services (Render, Google Apps Script, Netlify, Google Docs) operate on their respective platforms. No user credentials or session tokens are stored within this central portal.
